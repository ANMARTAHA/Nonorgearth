Overview

the laws, not the lecture

Group 2 in one pass — identity, chemistry, and the three medically important members.

IDENTITY

  • Group 2 = Be, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba, Ra (s-block, with the alkali metals)
  • 2 valence electrons, ns² configuration
  • Always lose both electrons → stable M²⁺ cation
  • Too reactive to exist free in nature — found as cations in minerals

GROUP TREND

  • Electropositivity increases down the group (Mg → Ba)
  • Hydroxide solubility & thermal stability increase down the group
  • Oxides: general formula MO, generally basic
  • Peroxides exist for Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba — not for Be
  • Hydroxides act as bases except Be(OH)₂

HALIDES

  • Halides of Mg/Ca/Sr/Ba are ionic, sparingly soluble in water
  • MgCl₂, MgBr₂, MgI₂ are hygroscopic — absorb moisture
  • Anhydrous CaCl₂ — strong water affinity → used as a desiccant

BERYLLIUM — TOXIC, NOT THERAPEUTIC

  • Extremely poisonous — negligible clinical use
  • Inhalation → Chronic Beryllium Disease (CBD)
  • Skin contact with soluble salts → severe irritation

MAGNESIUM — THE COFACTOR

  • Cofactor for 300+ enzymes; activates ATP (Mg-ATP); stabilises DNA/RNA; core of chlorophyll
  • Most Mg salts soluble → laxative; Mg(OH)₂ suspension = milk of magnesia (antacid)
  • IV/IM MgSO₄ = emergency drug for arrhythmia and eclampsia
  • Absorbed in ileum/colon, excreted by kidneys — reduce dose in renal impairment
  • Reduces absorption of iron, nitrofurantoin, proguanil, ACE inhibitors, aspirin, penicillamine

CALCIUM — THE MESSENGER

  • Most abundant inorganic element in the body (~1000 g); 99% in bone/teeth
  • Regulated by PTH — raises blood Ca²⁺ via bone resorption, gut uptake, renal reabsorption
  • Deficiency links: osteoporosis, hypertension, obesity
  • Renal osteodystrophy: failed kidneys → low calcitriol → hypocalcemia + hyperphosphatemia → PTH-driven bone loss
  • Higher dietary calcium (~1200 mg/day) is now linked to fewer oxalate kidney stones, reversing older belief
  • Calcium carbonate = 40% elemental Ca; take with food; pair with Mg salts to offset constipation
  • Severe acute hypocalcemia → slow IV 10% calcium gluconate, with ECG monitoring
